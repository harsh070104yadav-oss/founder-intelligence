# Research Methodology

## Philosophy

Do not collect information. Develop understanding of mechanisms: why companies succeed or fail, how distribution is built, how models evolve, what founders overlooked or understood.

## Source Hierarchy

1. **Tier 1 — Primary Issued Documents**: Regulatory filings (SEC 10-K/10-Q/8-K, SEBI, BSE/NSE), audited financials, official annual reports, investor presentations, earnings releases, shareholder letters, official company disclosures. These establish facts.
2. **Tier 2**: Earnings transcripts, reputable journalism, academic research, recognised industry reports.
3. **Tier 3**: Founder interviews, public talks, biographies, shareholder letters, expert commentary.
4. **Tier 4 — Public Sentiment**: X (Twitter), Reddit, forums, app reviews, social media. Used only to generate hypotheses, surface customer reactions, and identify narratives. Never independently establish financial or strategic claims.

Every material claim must have a source. Financial figures specify period, currency, metric type (revenue vs GMV vs ARR), and distinguish actual vs guidance.

## How Sentiment (especially X) Is Used

- Search X for company names, product reactions, founder posts, controversies, and recurring customer complaints or praise.
- Extract recurring themes (pricing, trust, retention, doctor experience, privacy, fulfilment).
- Treat all sentiment as unverified. Cross-check any factual assertion against Tier 1 documents.
- Include notable sentiment patterns in daily briefings under "Questions" or "Limitations," or as context for distribution and product insights.
- Do not let volume of posts or influencer opinions override official disclosures.

## Categories

- Company intelligence
- Corporate documents
- Business models
- Distribution and growth
- Technology and opportunities
- Industry structure
- Founder decisions
- Failures and mistakes

## Depth

Prefer deep analysis of fewer companies over shallow coverage. Track evolution over multiple years. Update profiles with new disclosures. Always begin with primary issued documents.

## Daily Process

1. Check for new primary issued documents (filings, earnings, investor presentations).
2. Retrieve and analyse those documents.
3. Supplement with Tier 2 and Tier 3 sources.
4. Scan X and other public sentiment for relevant reactions and themes (labelled as such).
5. Extract lessons, connect to prior research, and identify new hypotheses.
6. Update the knowledge base.
7. Publish the daily briefing.

## Accuracy

Never fabricate numbers. Mark unverified claims. Summarise rather than reproduce copyrighted material. Sentiment never overrides issued documents.
