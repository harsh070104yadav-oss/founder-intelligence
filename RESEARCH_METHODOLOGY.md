# Research Methodology

## Philosophy

Do not collect information. Develop understanding of mechanisms: why companies succeed or fail, how distribution is built, how models evolve, what founders overlooked or understood.

## Source Hierarchy

1. **Tier 1**: Regulatory filings (SEC 10-K/10-Q, SEBI, annual reports), audited financials, official IR materials, government data.
2. **Tier 2**: Earnings transcripts, reputable journalism, academic research.
3. **Tier 3**: Founder interviews, talks, shareholder letters.
4. **Tier 4**: Forums, social media — hypotheses only.

Every material claim must have a source. Financial figures specify period, currency, metric type (revenue vs GMV vs ARR), and distinguish actual vs guidance.

## Categories

- Company intelligence
- Corporate documents
- Business models
- Distribution and growth
- Technology and opportunities
- Industry structure
- Founder decisions
- Failures and mistakes

## Depth

Prefer deep analysis of fewer companies over shallow coverage. Track evolution over multiple years. Update profiles with new disclosures.

## Daily Process

Identify new disclosures or deep historical cases → retrieve primary docs → analyze → extract lessons → connect to prior research → update knowledge base → publish daily briefing.

## Accuracy

Never fabricate numbers. Mark unverified claims. Summarize rather than reproduce copyrighted material.
