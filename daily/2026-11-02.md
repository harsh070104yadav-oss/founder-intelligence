## 2. Companies studied

### Hims & Hers Health (HIMS)
- **Background**: Founded by Andrew Dudum. Consumer-first telehealth + pharmacy platform focused on subscription access across specialties. Mix of compounded and branded products; expanding internationally.
- **Business model**: DTC digital acquisition + subscription + owned or controlled fulfillment/pharmacy. Personalization as claimed differentiator for adherence and retention. AI positioned to create a unified, end-to-end care experience.
- **Financial performance** (Q2 2026 primary disclosures): Revenue $753.2 million (+38% YoY). Nearly 3 million subscribers. Full-year 2026 guidance $3.1–3.3 billion. International ≥$600 million near breakeven while scaling. Gross margin pressure from mix. 2030 ambition ≥$6.5B revenue and ≥$1.3B adj. EBITDA.
- **Leadership thinking and future vision** (Q2 2026 earnings call): Dudum — first-of-its-kind health experience delivering world-class care globally at reasonable price. Trust earned over 9 years, not bought. Elshenawy — “We are making AI load-bearing not decorative.” Closed-loop data creates compounding improvement. Clinicians stay in the loop. Accept near-term volatility for long-term platform.
- **Important lessons**: Clear long-term vision invested through near-term costs. Closed-loop data and earned trust positioned as moats. Critical to distinguish stated strategy from revealed margin and regulatory pressures.

Sources: Hims & Hers Q2 2026 Earnings Call Transcript (Aug 10, 2026), Q2 earnings release and 10-Q.
