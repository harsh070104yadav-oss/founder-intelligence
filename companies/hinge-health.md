# Hinge Health (HNGE)

## Overview
Digital musculoskeletal (MSK) care platform that uses AI, computer vision, connected hardware, and selective clinician involvement to automate physical therapy and related care. Sold primarily to self-insured employers and health plans. Expanding into migraine (launched) and GI (integrated program targeted summer 2027; acquired Cylinder Health Aug 2026). Founded 2014 by Daniel Perez (CEO) and Gabriel Mecklenburg. Public since May 2025. Leadership transition: President Jim Pursley stepping down Jan 2027 to join Board; Aaryn Pure to lead commercial.

## Business Model
- Channel distribution via employers and health plans/partners (3,270 clients as of 30 Sep 2026, +28% YoY; 50+ plans, PBMs, TPAs).
- Revenue from engaged members / contracts, recognized ratably.
- High gross margins driven by automation of care delivery (GAAP 87%, non-GAAP 88%).
- LTM calculated billings as leading indicator of growth.
- Proprietary hardware (e.g., Enso) and AI tools (Robin care assistant) as vertical elements.
- Platform expansion into adjacent conditions leveraging existing client relationships rather than pure standalone builds. Core MSK remains largest by wide margin.

## Key Financials (primary, Q3 2026)
- Revenue: $235.0M (+52% YoY)
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 33%
- Free cash flow: $112.7M (48% margin; $120M ex one-time)
- LTM calculated billings: $960.3M (+54%)
- Clients: 3,270 (+28%)
- FY2026 guidance (raised): Revenue $879–881M (50% growth at midpoint); non-GAAP operating income $266–268M (30% margin midpoint)

## Leadership Thinking
- Vision: Use technology to scale and automate the delivery of care itself to achieve the triple aim (outcomes, experience, cost) at scale.
- Member-first principle embedded in design and decisions.
- Product is never finished; stubborn on vision, flexible on execution.
- Hardware + software both essential for true automation.
- Expand platform into adjacencies via existing channels rather than standalone builds. First-mover preferred partner status aids this.
- Complementary founder skills; emphasis on clear writing and pace-setting leadership.
- Explicit: Core MSK growing robustly and expected to remain largest for years; automation closes care gap.

## Stated vs Revealed
Stated automation and triple-aim focus match revealed high margins, strong FCF conversion, yield improvements from engagement, client growth, billings growth, and guidance raises. Multi-condition expansion is early but commercially path-aligned with existing relationships (multi-product access for millions of lives).

## Lessons
Channel-aligned distribution combined with care automation can produce durable high-margin growth and free cash flow in digital health. Outcomes and cost signals matter more than pure access. Platform leverage from existing trust is more efficient than pure category expansion.
