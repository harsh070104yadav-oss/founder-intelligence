# Apollo Hospitals Enterprise

Integrated healthcare group: hospitals (core high-margin clinical business), omnichannel pharmacy distribution (Apollo HealthCo), clinics/diagnostics, and digital platform (Apollo 24|7). Founded 1983 by Dr. Prathap C. Reddy. Family-led under MD Suneeta Reddy. Ongoing demerger of HealthCo targeted for listing ~Q4 FY27. Recent non-core divestiture of Cradle (maternity) and Fertility stakes to Kids Clinic India (Cloudnine) for ~₹1,550 crore EV (cash + retained ~9.9% equity stake + nominee director). Transaction closed early November 2026 per BSE disclosures; CCI approved.

## Key Leadership Thinking

- Founder-Chairman Dr. Prathap C. Reddy: Transformative period in healthcare. Emphasis on caring beyond curing, specialised capacity, clinical teams, and Health and Happiness@Home. Digital as connective layer across physical footprint. Technology (including AI) must work with human empathy for accessible, affordable care.
- MD Suneeta Reddy (Q2 FY27 commentary, earnings call 5 Nov 2026 and related interviews): Established hospitals sustain ~26% EBITDA margins; new beds (520 commissioned) create temporary dilution (~24% overall hospital margin) but expected to break even within ~1 year (one unit already at EBITDA breakeven). Capital allocation prioritises execution, operating performance, and discipline with explicit unit-economics path. On regulatory price caps (oncology drugs, hospital pricing): healthcare is a service-delivery model, not cost-plus; continues government engagement while protecting growth and margins. Demerger framed as pure business decision for distinct economics (~20% pharmacy/digital vs ~18% hospitals), efficiency, and shareholder value creation. Structural demand for quality beds intact; focus on metro/Tier-2 penetration. Preventive care, NCD management, and technology-enabled access as future priorities. Medical tourism supported by low-cost high-quality delivery.
- Sangita Reddy (AHLL Chairperson): Partnership with Cloudnine marks pivotal moment for women’s healthcare in India; combining clinical excellence with holistic care to create larger integrated mother-and-child platform.
- Clinical quality and operational efficiency remain priorities alongside scale. Revealed: continued capacity addition, demerger advancement, non-core specialty recycling into specialized operator while retaining strategic stake and board seat.

- Consolidated revenue: ₹7,615 crore (+23% YoY). EBITDA ₹1,191 crore (+29%). PAT ₹630 crore (+32%). Margin 15.6%.
- Healthcare services: ₹3,900 crore (+23%), occupancy 73% (established 76%, from 69%), IP volumes +14%, ARPP ₹1,86,880 (+8%). High-complexity specialties driving growth (Oncology +28% etc.). International patients +37%. New hospitals: ~₹135 crore revenue with ~₹35 crore EBITDA loss.
- Apollo HealthCo: ₹3,286 crore (+23%), EBITDA ₹193 crore. Pharmacy network 7,662 stores. Apollo 24|7 GMV ₹615 crore (+20%).
- H1 FY27: revenue ₹14,547 crore (+22%), PAT ₹1,211 crore (+33%).
- Expansion: ~5,800–6,000 beds over five years; remaining capex largely from internal accruals. New units breakeven path ~12 months.
- Transaction: AHLL transferred Cradle and Fertility to Cloudnine for ~₹1,550 crore EV; AHLL holds ~9.9% in combined entity (largest non-financial shareholder) with board representation. Combined platform 50+ centres.

Prior full-year context (FY2025-26): consolidated revenue ~₹25,229 crore (+16%).
