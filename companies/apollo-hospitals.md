# Apollo Hospitals Enterprise

Integrated healthcare group: hospitals (core high-margin clinical business), omnichannel pharmacy distribution (Apollo HealthCo), clinics/diagnostics, and digital platform (Apollo 24|7). Founded 1983 by Dr. Prathap C. Reddy. Family-led under MD Suneeta Reddy. Ongoing demerger of HealthCo targeted for listing ~Q4 FY27. Recent non-core divestiture of Cradle (fertility) and ASPL stakes to Kids Clinic India (Cloudnine) for ~₹1,550 crore (cash + retained equity stake).

## Key Leadership Thinking

- Founder-Chairman Dr. Prathap C. Reddy: Transformative period in healthcare. Emphasis on caring beyond curing, specialised capacity, clinical teams, and Health and Happiness@Home. Digital as connective layer across physical footprint.
- MD Suneeta Reddy (Q2 FY27 commentary, earnings call 5 Nov 2026 and related interviews): Established hospitals sustain ~26% EBITDA margins; new beds (520 commissioned) create temporary dilution (~24% overall hospital margin) but expected to break even within ~1 year (one unit already at EBITDA breakeven). Capital allocation prioritises execution, operating performance, and discipline with explicit unit-economics path. On regulatory price caps (oncology drugs, hospital pricing): healthcare is a service-delivery model, not cost-plus; continues government engagement while protecting growth and margins. Demerger framed as pure business decision for distinct economics (~20% pharmacy/digital vs ~18% hospitals), efficiency, and shareholder value creation. Structural demand for quality beds intact; focus on metro/Tier-2 penetration. Preventive care, NCD management, and technology-enabled access as future priorities. Medical tourism supported by low-cost high-quality delivery.
- Clinical quality and operational efficiency remain priorities alongside scale. Revealed: continued capacity addition, demerger advancement, non-core asset recycling.
