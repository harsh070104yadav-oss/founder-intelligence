# Teladoc Health

Global virtual care platform operating Integrated Care (primarily B2B) and BetterHelp (consumer mental health) segments. Illustrates post-COVID normalisation challenges for pure digital access models.

## Key Leadership Thinking (from Q3 2026 earnings materials — CEO Chuck Divita)

- Overarching objective: be the most trusted leader in virtual care, delivering outcomes that matter to patients, clients, and stakeholders.
- Strategic priorities: enhance Integrated Care solutions (Teladoc One, hybrid care, AI), leverage scaled mental health position, international growth, operational excellence.
- BetterHelp pivot: expanding insurance coverage for greater durability (27k weekly covered sessions / ~$140M annualised). Accept near-term cash-pay pressure from reduced advertising.
- Emphasises disciplined execution; “more work ahead” while claiming traction.
