# Teladoc Health

Global virtual care platform operating Integrated Care (primarily B2B) and BetterHelp (consumer mental health) segments. Illustrates post-COVID normalisation challenges for pure digital access models and the mechanics of pivoting cash-pay to insurance-reimbursed durability.

## Key Leadership Thinking (from Q3 2026 earnings materials — CEO Chuck Divita; subsequent interview)

- Overarching objective: be the most trusted leader in virtual care, delivering outcomes that matter to patients, clients, and stakeholders. 2026 framed as “execution year.”
- Strategic priorities: enhance Integrated Care solutions (Teladoc One, hybrid care, AI), leverage scaled mental health position via insurance durability, international growth, operational excellence.
- BetterHelp pivot: expanding insurance coverage for greater durability (Q3: 27k weekly covered sessions / ~$140M annualised; 36% of new U.S. users from insurance). Accept near-term cash-pay pressure from reduced advertising. Goal: sequential insurance growth and ≥$200M annualised exit run rate by end-2027.
- Capital allocation: M&A as accelerator (UpLift for insurance entry; Teledyne for hybrid). Disciplined cost control; proactive debt management for 2027 convertible notes.
- Explicit future views: Cash-pay faces persistent affordability/competition; insurance removes friction for focus on therapy. Virtual care as catalyst amid system capacity constraints. AI/hybrid to expand efficiency.
- Stated vs revealed: Stated durability focus matches revealed insurance ramp and margin expansion via advertising pullback. Overall revenue decline and cash-pay erosion reveal the cost of the pivot and normalisation. Not heroised; depends on capacity scaling and offsetting decline.
