# Hims & Hers Health

Consumer-first health and wellness platform combining telehealth and pharmacy fulfillment. Focus on personalization, subscription, and international expansion.

## Key Leadership Thinking (from Q2 2026 earnings call — Andrew Dudum & Mo Elshenawy)

- Vision of proactive, preventative, always-on care that is globally affordable.
- “We are making AI load-bearing not decorative.” Closed-loop data (intake → treatment → follow-up → outcomes) as the hard-to-replicate advantage.
- Trust earned over 9 years by listening and delivering outcomes; not something you can buy.
- Long-term oriented; near-term GAAP volatility is acceptable.
- Customers drive category expansion. Personalization claimed to improve adherence and engagement.
- Clinicians remain in the loop; AI elevates by removing routine work.
- 2030 targets: ≥$6.5B revenue, ≥$1.3B adjusted EBITDA.

## Latest Financial Context (Q2 2026)

- Revenue $753.2M (+38% YoY).
- Nearly 3 million subscribers.
- 2026 guidance $3.1–3.3B.
- International expected ≥$600M in 2026, near breakeven while scaling.
- Gross margin pressure from branded products and international mix.
