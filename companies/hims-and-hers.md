# Hims & Hers Health

Consumer-first health and wellness platform combining telehealth and pharmacy fulfillment. Focus on personalization, subscription, international expansion, and (increasingly) branded manufacturer partnerships.

## Key Leadership Thinking (shareholder letters, earnings calls, interviews — Andrew Dudum)

- Vision of proactive, preventative, always-on care that is globally affordable and customer-centered. “Everyone is different, and today’s standard systems can’t keep up.”
- Closed-loop data (intake → treatment → follow-up → outcomes) as compounding advantage. “We are making AI load-bearing not decorative.” Clinicians remain in the loop.
- Trust earned over years by listening and delivering outcomes; not something you can buy via advertising.
- Long-term oriented; near-term GAAP volatility and margin compression from mix are acceptable.
- Customers drive category expansion. Personalization claimed to improve adherence and engagement.
- Capital allocation framework: short marketing payback, invest in capacity/technology/personalization/international/diagnostics. Strong cash generation supports concurrent investments.
- 2030 targets: ≥$6.5B revenue, ≥$1.3B adjusted EBITDA.
- Recent pragmatic adaptation: seamless branded GLP-1 access (Novo, Lilly) with non-compounding commitments for those products, while expanding proprietary specialties (e.g., peptides).

## Latest Financial Context (through Q2 2026)

- FY2025: ~$2.35B revenue (+59%), >2.5M subscribers, ~$318M Adjusted EBITDA.
- Q2 2026: $753.2M revenue (+38% YoY), nearly 3M subscribers. 2026 guidance $3.1–3.3B.
- International expected ≥$600M in 2026, near breakeven while scaling.
- Gross margin pressure from branded/weight-loss mix and international.
