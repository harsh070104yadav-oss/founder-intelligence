# Hims & Hers Health

Consumer-first health and wellness platform combining telehealth and pharmacy fulfillment. Focus on personalization, subscription, international expansion, and branded manufacturer partnerships.

## Key Leadership Thinking (shareholder letters, earnings calls, interviews — Andrew Dudum)

- Vision of proactive, preventative, always-on care that is globally affordable and customer-centered. “In healthcare, whoever owns the customer relationship wins.” Multiplier model across brands, platform, infrastructure, and AI.
- Closed-loop data (intake → treatment → follow-up → outcomes) as compounding advantage. AI “load-bearing not decorative”; clinicians remain in the loop. AI-native experience claimed to drive higher engagement.
- Trust earned over years by listening and delivering outcomes; not something you can buy via advertising.
- Long-term oriented; near-term GAAP volatility, margin compression from mix, and sequential subscriber dips from deliberate marketing restraint are acceptable to position for multi-year durability.
- Customers drive category expansion. Metabolic health as entry point that unlocks broader relationship.
- Capital allocation framework: short marketing payback, invest in capacity/technology/personalization/international/diagnostics. Strong cash generation supports concurrent investments. High bar into 2027.
- 2030 targets: ≥$6.5B revenue, ≥$1.3B adjusted EBITDA (aspirational).
- Pragmatic adaptation: seamless branded GLP-1 access (Novo, Lilly) with non-compounding commitments for those products, while expanding proprietary specialties (peptides, TRT).
