# GoodRx Holdings

Consumer platform for prescription price transparency, savings, telehealth, manufacturer-sponsored affordability programs (Pharma Direct), and condition-specific subscriptions. Founded 2011. CEO Wendy Barnes (from early 2025); founder Doug Hirsch remains a director.

## Key Leadership Thinking (Wendy Barnes)

- Two strategic priorities: Pharma Direct (manufacturer consumer-direct pricing programs, strong GLP-1 contribution) and subscriptions (retention and condition-specific access).
- Explicit mix-shift trade-off: accept near-term pressure on legacy prescription-transaction revenue to improve durability and redirect investment.
- Pharma Direct positioned as evolving the company into a digital storefront / consumer access platform for manufacturers, with multi-year runway through 2030–31 (including non-GLP-1).
- Capital allocation: invest in the business first; share repurchases when viewed as accretive; disciplined cost control.
- Acknowledges structural headwinds from retail pharmacy landscape changes.
