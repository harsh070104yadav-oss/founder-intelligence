# Progyny

Fertility and family-building benefits management company. Employer-channel distribution with outcomes-focused design (Smart Cycle) and selective specialist network + Care Advocates.

## Key Leadership Thinking (Pete Anevski)

- Entered because of the scale of the problem (1 in 6) and lack of a real solution. Patients were going into debt for a deeply personal need.
- Mission-focused leadership: clear North Star, right people.
- Differentiator is publishing clinical outcomes for every member (third-party validated) and delivering client-level reporting.
- Focus on actual utilization and total cost of care, not just eligible lives.
- Expanding into smaller employers (Progyny Select) and adjacent needs (menopause, midlife).
