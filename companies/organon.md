# Organon & Co.

Women’s health-focused global pharmaceutical company (spun from Merck) with portfolio including Nexplanon contraceptive implant, fertility products, biosimilars, and established brands. Pending all-cash acquisition by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries at $14.00 per share (shareholder approval July 2026; expected close early 2027, subject to remaining conditions including regulatory approvals). Companies operate independently until close. Earnings calls suspended during the process.

## Key Leadership Thinking (limited by merger process)

- Interim CEO Joseph Morrissey frames women’s health as a strategic lever for growth and sustainability: tremendous unmet need and opportunity to fill access gaps and support women at all stages of life. Diverse portfolio (women’s health-specific + chronic disease + biosimilars) integrates into an ecosystem supporting overall health. Observed growth in fertility and contraception alongside chronic portfolios when working collaboratively with healthcare systems.
- Capital allocation: Pursuit of full sale to Sun Pharma rather than independent long-term execution amid product headwinds and leverage. Prior focus on portfolio optimisation (Jada divestiture completed early 2026) and control remediation following earlier Nexplanon U.S. sales practices investigation (no restatement; enhanced controls). Selective licensing (Miudella hormone-free contraceptive; anticipated late 2026 commercial availability).
- Stated vs revealed: Portfolio focus on women’s health consistent with positioning. Revealed: product demand headwinds (lower U.S. Nexplanon), high leverage, and strategic exit via acquisition. Public independent narrative currently muted by deal process. Not heroised; category continuity under new ownership unproven.
